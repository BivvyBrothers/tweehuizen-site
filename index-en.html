Features

What can TweeHuizen do?

No more scattered messages. No more discussions about who had which days. Everything in one place, visible to everyone.

A care schedule that keeps up

Set up your base schedule once: alternating weeks, a weekly pattern or custom. Changes show up right away, for everyone involved.

Plan together without the hassle

Propose swapping a day or splitting a holiday. The other parent gets a notification and responds with one tap.

Never forget things again

Add activities with a list of things to bring. The parent doing the drop-off (and the child too) gets a reminder in good time.

Fair insight

See at a glance how many days the children have spent with whom: per month, quarter or year.

The whole family can join

Not just the parents. Step-parents and grandparents can be invited too, each with their own permissions.

Private by design

Your data is there for your family. No ad tracking and no ads in the app, and our database is hosted by Supabase in the EU. Profile photos are only visible to your own family. Read more in our privacy policy (in Dutch).

Get started

How it works

Within 5 minutes you are ready to plan together.

  1. Create a family

    Download the app, create an account and add your children.

  2. Set up the base schedule

    Choose your regular care schedule: alternating weeks or your own pattern.

  3. Invite the other parent

    Share an invitation code. From then on you plan together.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

The most common questions, short and clear.

Can the other parent join?

Yes. You share an invitation code and the other parent creates their own account, linked to the same family.

Do children count as a seat in the subscription?

No. Child profiles are always free. Only adults take up a seat.

What happens if I no longer have a subscription?

Your base schedule and family details are kept. Premium features (proposals, reminders, export) are paused until you subscribe again.
